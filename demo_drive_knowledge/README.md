# Parallax.inc Drive knowledge demo

This is a generated demonstration knowledge base for Parallax.inc, with connected policies, people, project, support, customer, vendor, and operating records. Employee and customer records are generated sample data. Work email addresses use the reserved `example.test` domain. The internal tax reference is deliberately not a valid PAN or GST identifier.

## Upload and connect

1. In Google Drive, use **New → Folder upload** and upload the complete `Parallax.inc Knowledge Demo` folder next to this guide. It contains 15 plain-text and CSV documents.
2. In Parallax, open **Knowledge sources**, choose **Connected sources**, and connect the Google account that contains the uploaded folder.
3. Select `Parallax.inc Knowledge Demo` from the folder list.
4. Ask a question below. Parallax should retrieve the relevant passage and cite the original Drive document.

## Sample questions

- What is Parallax.inc's internal tax reference, and is it a PAN?
- Who leads Engineering, and what is their employee directory entry?
- How many annual leave days are available, and who approves an exception?
- What is blocking the Atlas launch and who owns the access review?
- Who owns PX-SUP-1042, and what is its current status?
- How many accounts are marked At Risk or Needs Attention?
- What were October recurring revenue and net revenue retention?
- Which vendors have a high risk tier?

The Drive connector reads Google Docs and plain-text/CSV files directly inside the selected folder. It searches file contents at query time and cites the original Drive file. It does not search subfolders or PDFs.
