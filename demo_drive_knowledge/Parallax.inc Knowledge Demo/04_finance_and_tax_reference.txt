PARALLAX.INC — FINANCE OPERATIONS REFERENCE

Finance owner: Mira Sen, Chief Financial Officer.
Reporting currency: INR. Fiscal year: April–March.
Expense approval: Team leads approve expenses up to INR 50,000. Department heads approve INR 50,001–250,000. The CFO approves any larger spend or unbudgeted commitment.
Purchase orders: A purchase order is required before a vendor begins work. Finance matches the PO, invoice, and delivery confirmation before payment.
Reimbursements: Submit receipts within 30 days through the expense workflow.
Monthly close: Department cost owners certify their lines by the third business day; Finance publishes the consolidated review by the seventh business day.

Internal tax reference: PXINC-TAX-REF-2026. This is a demonstration reference and is not a PAN or other statutory identifier. Use approved finance records for any real filing or transaction.

Vendor and contract records are maintained in 11_vendor_register.csv. Customer accounts and renewals are recorded in 08_customer_accounts.csv.
