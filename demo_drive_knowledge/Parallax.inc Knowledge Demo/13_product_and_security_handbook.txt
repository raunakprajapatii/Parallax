PARALLAX.INC — PRODUCT, SECURITY, AND CUSTOMER DATA HANDBOOK

Customer data: Use customer workspaces only for the purpose agreed in the contract. Do not copy production customer content into a personal account or an unapproved demo workspace.

Access control: Workspace access follows least privilege. The workspace owner approves role changes; Security reviews privileged roles quarterly. Shared links should be limited to the intended audience and revoked when no longer needed.

Incident response: Report suspected account compromise, unintended disclosure, or data loss to Security immediately. Security assigns an incident lead, preserves relevant logs, and coordinates customer communication with Legal and Customer Success.

Data retention: Customer admins configure workspace retention within their contracted plan. Legal holds override normal deletion schedules. Contact Security for a deletion or export request that affects a legal hold.

Product release: Product owns release criteria, Engineering owns implementation and rollback readiness, and Customer Success owns customer readiness. A launch with an open critical access-control finding requires executive risk acceptance.

Atlas launch gate: No production customer data may be loaded into Atlas until the permission-matrix review is signed off and the privacy review is complete.
